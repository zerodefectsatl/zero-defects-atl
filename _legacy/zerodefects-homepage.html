04

XPEL Paint Protection Film

Paint Protection Film (PPF — also called clear bra, clear film, or clear paint film) is a urethane film applied to painted surfaces to protect against stone chips, bug splatters, and minor abrasions. It is OEM approved by virtually all car manufacturers and used on everything from exotic sports cars to aircraft and motorcycles.

Today's PPF is highly conformable and optically clear. XPEL's Pro Series features a multi-layered self-healing top coat capable of reforming itself after being scuffed or scratched — maintaining its clarity for more than ten years. PPF is most commonly applied to forward-facing high-impact areas but can also cover a full vehicle for maximum protection.

At Zero Defects, we completed a week-long XPEL factory training certification at their headquarters in San Antonio, Texas. We use all current techniques and practices to ensure the highest quality install possible.

  • ✓ Self-healing top coat — scuffs and light scratches disappear with heat
  • ✓ OEM approved by virtually all car manufacturers
  • ✓ XPEL factory-trained & certified — week-long course in San Antonio, TX
  • ✓ Computer-cut templates for seamless, precision fitment
  • ✓ XPEL Ultimate Plus (gloss) or XPEL Stealth (satin matte) — your choice
  • ✓ 10-year XPEL manufacturer warranty
